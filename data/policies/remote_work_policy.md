# Remote Work Policy

Document ID: HR-REM | Version: 2.0 | Effective: February 1, 2026 | Owner: People Operations

## REM-1: Purpose and Scope

Larkspur Analytics supports flexible working arrangements that allow employees to do their best work while staying connected to their teams. This policy sets out who may work remotely, what is expected of remote employees, and what support the company provides. It applies to all regular employees. Roles that require a physical presence, such as facilities and hardware lab staff, are designated as on-site roles and are not eligible for remote arrangements.

## REM-2: Work Arrangements

The company recognizes three arrangements. Hybrid employees work from a company office at least two days per week, with Tuesday as the shared anchor day for all teams. Fully remote employees work from home and visit an office only for scheduled team events. On-site employees work from the office five days per week. An employee's arrangement is recorded in the HR portal and may be changed with the approval of their manager and People Operations. New employees are expected to follow a hybrid arrangement during their first 90 days unless they were hired into a fully remote role.

## REM-3: Core Hours and Availability

All employees, regardless of location, are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedules to suit their circumstances, provided they work their contracted hours and attend scheduled meetings. Remote employees should keep their calendar and chat status up to date and respond to messages within four business hours during the working day. Cameras are encouraged but not required in meetings.

## REM-4: Equipment and Home Office Stipend

The company provides every employee with a laptop and standard peripherals. Fully remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 600 dollars to purchase furniture or equipment such as a desk, chair, or monitor. The stipend is available after 30 days of employment and is claimed through the expense tool with receipts. In addition, fully remote employees receive an internet allowance of 50 dollars per month, paid through payroll. Company equipment remains company property and must be returned within 14 days of the end of employment.

## REM-5: Security Requirements

Remote employees must follow the same information security standards as office-based staff. Company devices must connect through the corporate VPN whenever company systems are accessed from outside an office. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN enabled. Screens must be locked when a device is unattended, and confidential conversations must not be held where they can be overheard. Company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT Service Desk within one hour of discovery.

## REM-6: Working from Other Locations

Employees may work temporarily from a location other than their registered home address within their country of employment for up to 30 days per calendar year, with notice to their manager. Working from another country raises tax, legal, and security questions and is limited to 20 working days per calendar year. International remote work requires written approval from People Operations at least 21 days before travel and is not permitted in countries on the restricted list maintained by the Security team.

## REM-7: Performance and Communication

Remote and hybrid employees are held to the same performance standards as on-site employees and are evaluated on outcomes, not on hours visible online. Managers are expected to hold a one-on-one meeting with each direct report at least every two weeks. Teams should document decisions in writing so that colleagues in other locations and time zones are not disadvantaged.

## REM-8: In-Person Events

Fully remote employees are expected to attend two company-wide gatherings per year, held in spring and autumn. Travel to these events is paid for by the company and booked according to the Expense Reimbursement Policy. Team-level offsites are optional and funded from departmental budgets.

## REM-9: Changes and Revocation

A remote work arrangement is not a contractual entitlement. The company may modify or end an arrangement with 30 days of written notice where business needs change or where performance or security expectations are not being met. Questions should be directed to People Operations at people@larkspur.example.
