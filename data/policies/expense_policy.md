# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: FIN-EXP | Version: 2.1 | Effective: March 1, 2026 | Owner: Finance

## EXP-1: Purpose and Scope

This policy explains which business expenses Larkspur Analytics will reimburse, the limits that apply, and how to submit a claim. It applies to all employees who incur costs on behalf of the company. The guiding principle is simple: spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business, and supported by documentation.

## EXP-2: Submitting a Claim

All expense claims must be submitted through the Ledgerline expense tool within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require approval from the Finance Director and may be denied. An itemized receipt is required for every expense of 25 dollars or more. Credit card statements are not accepted in place of receipts. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll run, provided they are approved at least five business days before the payroll date.

## EXP-3: Air Travel and Lodging

Flights must be booked in economy class through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Premium economy is permitted for flights with a scheduled duration of more than six hours. Business class requires written pre-approval from a Vice President. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 220 dollars per night before taxes in standard cities and up to 320 dollars per night in designated high-cost cities, which are listed in the travel portal. Ground transportation should use the most economical reasonable option; ride-share services are reimbursable, while luxury or premium vehicle tiers are not.

## EXP-4: Meals and Per Diem

While travelling on company business, employees may claim actual meal costs up to 75 dollars per day, including tax and tip. Meals are reimbursed based on receipts, not as a flat allowance. Alcohol is not reimbursable as part of travel meals. Team meals and client entertainment are covered separately: client meals are reimbursable up to 120 dollars per person and require the names of all attendees and the business purpose to be recorded on the claim. Team celebration meals require prior approval from the department head and are limited to 50 dollars per person.

## EXP-5: Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed: personal entertainment such as in-room movies, minibar purchases, traffic and parking fines, airline upgrades purchased without approval, spouse or companion travel, personal grooming, gym fees while travelling, and lost personal property. Expenses that lack a required receipt are also non-reimbursable unless a missing receipt declaration is approved by Finance. Employees are limited to two missing receipt declarations per calendar year.

## EXP-6: Approval Thresholds

Claims are routed for approval based on the total value of the claim. Claims up to 500 dollars are approved by the employee's direct manager. Claims between 500 and 2,500 dollars require approval from the department head. Claims above 2,500 dollars require approval from both the department head and the Finance Director. No employee may approve their own expenses, and managers may not approve expenses for a meal or event that they themselves attended unless they are the most senior person present.

## EXP-7: Corporate Card

Employees who travel more than four times per year may apply for a corporate card. The corporate card may be used only for business expenses, and personal charges are prohibited. Card transactions must still be reconciled in Ledgerline with receipts within 30 days. Cards with unreconciled transactions older than 60 days will be suspended until reconciliation is complete. A lost or stolen card must be reported to Finance and the card issuer within 24 hours.

## EXP-8: Software and Subscriptions

Software purchases and recurring subscriptions must not be bought on a personal card and expensed. They must be requested through the IT procurement form so that security and licensing can be reviewed. One-off purchases of books, courses, and conference tickets are reimbursable under the professional development budget of 1,500 dollars per employee per calendar year, with manager approval.

## EXP-9: Audits and Violations

Finance audits a random sample of claims each quarter. Employees must retain original receipts for 90 days after reimbursement. Submitting false or inflated claims is a serious violation and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Questions should be sent to expenses@larkspur.example.
