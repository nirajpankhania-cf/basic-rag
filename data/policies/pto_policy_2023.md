# Paid Time Off Policy

Document ID: HR-PTO | Version: 1.0 | Effective: January 1, 2023 | Owner: People Operations

## PTO-1: Purpose and Scope

Larkspur Analytics believes that regular time away from work is essential to the health, creativity, and long-term performance of our people. This policy describes how paid time off (PTO) is earned, requested, and used. It applies to all regular full-time employees. Part-time employees who work at least 20 hours per week earn PTO on a pro-rated basis according to their scheduled hours. Contractors, interns, and temporary staff are not covered by this policy and should refer to the terms of their individual agreements.

## PTO-2: Accrual Rates

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per calendar year, earned at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Accrual begins on the first day of employment, and new hires may use PTO as soon as it has been earned. After five years of continuous service, the annual accrual rate increases to 20 days per year. Accrual rates are reviewed each year by People Operations, and PTO balances are visible at any time in the HR portal under the Time Away tab. Employees on unpaid leave do not accrue PTO for the duration of that leave.

## PTO-3: Requesting Time Off

All PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the employee's direct manager. Requests for more than three consecutive days require at least one week of advance notice. Requests for three days or fewer should be submitted as early as possible, and no later than two business days in advance. Managers are expected to respond to requests within three business days. Requests may be declined only for documented business reasons, such as a critical release window or insufficient team coverage, and managers should work with the employee to find alternative dates.

## PTO-4: Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 10 days of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by June 30, after which they are forfeited. Any unused balance above the carryover limit is forfeited on December 31. Human Resources sends a balance reminder in October so that employees have time to plan. Exceptions to the carryover limit require written approval from the Vice President of People Operations and are granted only when business needs prevented the employee from taking scheduled time off.

## PTO-5: Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not reduce an employee's PTO balance. Full-time employees receive 5 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, available in full on January 1. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. Absences of more than three consecutive sick days require a note from a healthcare provider. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out at separation.

## PTO-6: Company Holidays

Larkspur Analytics observes 9 paid company holidays each year. The holiday calendar is published in the HR portal every November for the following year. Company holidays do not count against PTO balances. Employees who are required to work on a company holiday because of on-call duties receive a floating day off, which must be used within 60 days.

## PTO-7: Payout at Separation

When employment ends, accrued but unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck, up to a maximum of 15 days. Payout is calculated at the employee's base salary rate on their last day of employment. PTO may not be used to extend the final date of employment beyond the agreed notice period unless approved by People Operations.

## PTO-8: Parental Leave

Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Parental leave is separate from PTO and must be taken within 12 months of the event. Employees should notify their manager and People Operations at least 60 days before the expected start of leave where possible. PTO continues to accrue during paid parental leave.

## PTO-9: Questions and Policy Review

Questions about this policy should be directed to People Operations at people@larkspur.example. This policy is reviewed annually and may be updated at the company's discretion; employees will be notified of material changes at least 30 days before they take effect.
